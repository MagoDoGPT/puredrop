The Science

What's really in your water?

Tap water travels through ageing pipes and picks up invisible contaminants before it reaches your glass. PureDrop's multi-stage filtration eliminates what you can't see.

Microplastics

Studies show tap water contains up to 4,000 micro-particles per litre. PureDrop removes 99.8% of them.

Chlorine & Chemicals

Residual disinfectants alter taste and can irritate skin. Our activated carbon stage strips them entirely.

Essential Minerals Restored

Unlike reverse osmosis, PureDrop's final stage re-mineralises with calcium and magnesium for optimal taste.
